RING DIAMETER INSTRUMENT

Measure a ring
from a photo.

No calipers. No typing in numbers. Just the ring, a printed marker, and a camera held overhead.

  1. 01

    Print the marker

    Exactly 20 × 20 mm. Print at 100% / Actual Size — never "Fit to page".

  2. 02

    Verify the print

    Check the scale square on the printed page with a ruler. If it isn't exactly 20 mm, reprint.

  3. 03

    Place them together

    Ring flat, marker beside it, same flat surface, marker fully visible.

  4. 04

    Shoot from directly above

    Sharp focus, even light, minimal glare. Steep angles and blur will be rejected.
